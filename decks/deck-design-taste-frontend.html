Anti-default discipline.

Override the LLM reflex. Every default below has a context-aware replacement.

AvoidAI-purple gradients

No auto purple button glows. No mesh gradients on a centered hero.

Reach forOne saturated accent

Neutral base (Zinc / Slate / Stone) + a single high-contrast accent.

AvoidInter + slate-900

The default tells. Inter is OK only when the brief explicitly wants neutral.

Reach forGeist · Outfit · Cabinet Grotesk

Sans display fonts are the right default. Black is not "boring."

AvoidBeige + brass + espresso

The premium-consumer AI default. Every cookware, every wellness, every artisan brief.

Reach forRotated palettes

Cobalt+cream · forest+amber · terracotta+slate · black+tan · monochrome+pop.

AvoidCentered hero over mesh

Avoided at VARIANCE > 4. Split, asymmetric, scroll-pinned instead.

Reach forSplit-screen + real photography

50/50, left-content / right-asset, or asymmetric white-space.

Hero discipline.

The hero is a single moment, not a feature list. Max 4 text elements. Fits in the initial viewport. Top padding ≤ 6rem.

Hero stack (max 4)
Eyebrow · Headline · Sub · CTA

Pick ZERO or ONE small element above the headline. Subtext max 20 words, max 4 lines. CTAs: 1 primary + at most 1 secondary.

Viewport 100dvh

Never h-screen. iOS Safari jumps the bar.

Top padding pt-24 cap

More floats content halfway down. Increase scale instead.

Banned in hero

Tiny tagline below CTAs · trust micro-strip · pricing teaser · feature bullets · avatar row. All move below.

Trust logos

"Used by" wall belongs UNDER the hero. Real SVGs, not text wordmarks.

Eyebrow restraint.

The single most-violated rule. Max one eyebrow per three sections. This deck has 11 slides → 3 eyebrows total, and you've now seen all of them.

Layout diversification.

Once a layout family appears, it's used at most once on the page. The skill itself enforces this — every slide of this deck uses a different family.

Section-layout repetition ban. A landing page with 8 sections must use at least 4 different families.

Zigzag cap. Max 2 consecutive "left-image + right-text" rows. The 3rd is a Pre-Flight Fail.

Bento cell-count rule. 3 items → 3 cells. 5 items → 5 cells. Never paste a blank tile.

Bento background diversity. At least 2-3 cells in a multi-cell grid need real visual variation: an image, a tint, a pattern.

Split-header ban. Left-big-headline + right-floaty-paragraph as a section header — banned by default. Stack vertically instead.

Marquee max-one-per-page. Horizontal kinetic strips are fine once. Two reads as lazy filler.

Color calibration.

One palette per project. One accent. Saturation under 80% by default. Once chosen, it's locked across every section.

The Lila ruleban

The "AI purple / blue glow" aesthetic is the saturated default. No auto button glows. No random neon gradients.

Premium-consumer beige+brassban

The whole cookware/wellness/artisan default palette. Banned unless the brief explicitly names those colors.

Section accent fluctuationban

Warm-grey site doesn't get a blue CTA in section 7. Lock the accent. Audit every component.

Theme inversion mid-pageban

Pick light, dark, or auto at page level. No light-cream section sandwiched in a dark layout.
